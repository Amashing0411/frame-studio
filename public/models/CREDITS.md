## People (Phase 1 of design/overhaul-plan.md)

"Characters PSX" by elbolilloduro (https://elbolilloduro.itch.io/characters-psx), version 1.2 (rigged on
the Mixamo skeleton), rebuilt in Blender by `tools/blender/build_psx_people.py` into `public/assets/people/`: bones
renamed for the engine, T-pose rest, textures at 256 px; five of the six unrigged monsters rigged with a lent skeleton.
Used with the authors' permission (direct messages to the project owner, October 2026). Animation clips: the Quaternius
Universal Animation Library (CC0) retargeted onto the Mixamo rig. The earlier Quaternius cast is in
`archive/frame-studio-people-v1.zip` and commit 74d24a2.
